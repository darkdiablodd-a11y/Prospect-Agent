# Nightshift Labs Caller Pilot

## Objective

Book a 15-minute workflow review between a qualified local business decision
maker and Nightshift Labs. The caller is not expected to sell or explain a
technical implementation.

## Pilot limits

- Use 10–15 prospects approved by Nightshift Labs.
- Make live human calls only. No prerecorded or AI-generated voice calls.
- Use only the approved business phone numbers supplied by Prospect.
- Do not copy, resell, enrich, or reuse the prospect list.
- Stop calling immediately when a business asks not to be contacted.
- Return same-day notes for every attempt.

## Approved opening

Hi, this is [caller name] calling on behalf of Nightshift Labs here in
Charlotte. Could you point me to the owner or the person responsible for how
new customer inquiries and appointments are handled?

## Decision-maker conversation

Hi [name], I’ll be brief. Nightshift Labs helps local service businesses respond
to inquiries faster, qualify leads, schedule appointments, and follow up
without adding repetitive work for the team.

We noticed [approved observation from Prospect]. I wanted to ask: when calls or
website inquiries arrive after hours or during a busy period, what normally
happens?

[Listen and record the answer.]

That is exactly the type of workflow we review. Would you be open to a
15-minute conversation with Nightshift Labs to see where an AI-assisted intake
process could support your existing team?

## If they ask what Nightshift Labs does

Nightshift Labs builds practical AI systems for inquiry response, lead
qualification, appointment scheduling, follow-up, and customer reactivation.
The goal is to support the current team and reduce missed opportunities—not
replace the people already handling customers.

## Common objections

**We are not interested.**  
Understood. I’ll mark that and make sure Nightshift Labs does not follow up.

**We already have software for that.**  
That makes sense. The review is specifically about how the tools connect and
what still requires manual follow-up. If the current workflow is already
covered, the conversation will confirm that quickly.

**Send information.**  
Absolutely. What is the best business email, and who should the note be
addressed to?

**We are too busy.**  
That is usually when the workflow is most useful. Would a 15-minute time early
next week be easier?

**How much does it cost?**  
The price depends on the workflow and systems involved. The first conversation
is a no-cost 15-minute review so Nightshift Labs can determine whether there is
a useful fit.

## Required call report

For every attempt, return:

- business name;
- date and local time;
- outcome: no answer, gatekeeper, decision maker, callback, meeting, not
  interested, or do not call;
- decision-maker name and role when voluntarily provided;
- direct business email when voluntarily provided;
- current inquiry-handling answer;
- objection or concern;
- agreed next step;
- meeting date and time;
- concise notes.

## Hiring interview

Before a pilot, ask each candidate for:

1. a 30–60 second voice sample using the approved opening;
2. examples of B2B agency or local-service calling;
3. how they document every call and handle opt-outs;
4. whether they use a U.S. caller ID and which dialer they use;
5. confirmation that no subcontractor will receive the list;
6. confirmation that they will use live human calls only;
7. their price for a 10–15 prospect pilot with up to two attempts per prospect.

Nightshift Labs must approve the candidate, price, voice sample, script, and
pilot list before any prospect data is shared.
